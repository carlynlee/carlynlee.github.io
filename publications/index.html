Publications

Papers from my work at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory and with collaborators, on deep space communications, autonomous robotics, and space system analysis. Links go to the publisher or an open copy where one is available.

  1. 2026
    Measured WAN Traffic Reduction from Federated Edge AI in a Distributed Cosmic-Ray Sensor Network
    C. Lee, H. Newman, Y. Wu, S. Axani
    INDIS 2026 workshop, SC26 Supercomputing Conference, Chicago, IL (to appear)

    Smartphones acting as cosmic-ray detectors train a classifier in the browser and share only model weights instead of raw detections. At matched accuracy, federated training moved 3.2–11.4× less wide-area network traffic than centralizing the data.

  2. 2025
    Onboard AI and Federated Learning for Cosmic Ray Event Detection
    C. Lee
    Network Research Exhibition demonstration, SC25 Supercomputing Conference, St. Louis, MO

    Detecting cosmic ray events with machine learning onboard, training a shared model across distributed nodes with federated learning.

  3. 2025
    High Data Rates from the Outer Solar System
    K. Cheung, V. Vilnrotter, M. Sanchez-Net, C. Lee
    IEEE Aerospace Conference, Big Sky, MT

    How the Deep Space Network could support much higher data rates for future missions to the outer planets.

  4. 2022
    NeBula: TEAM CoSTAR's Robotic Autonomy Solution that Won Phase II of DARPA Subterranean Challenge
    A. Agha et al. (72 authors), including C. Lee
    Field Robotics, vol. 2, pp. 1432–1506 · arXiv preprint

    Team CoSTAR's autonomy architecture for robots exploring GPS- and comms-denied caves, mines and tunnels, which took first place at the DARPA SubT Urban Circuit.

  5. 2022
    Swarms of Pirates: Red Team Exercises Using Autonomous High-Speed Maneuvering Surface Vessels
    J. Vander Hook, W. Seto, V. Nguyen, Z. Hasnain, C. Lee, L. Gallagher, T. Halpin-Chan, V. Varahamurthy, M. Angulo
    Field Robotics, vol. 2, pp. 872–909

    Autonomous high-speed surface vessels used as a coordinated red-team swarm in Navy exercises: the largest fleet of autonomous maritime vehicles operated at the time.

  6. 2021
    Lunar Terrain Coverage Analysis Data Delivery Workflow
    C. Lee, M. Shaikh, C.H. Lee, D. Michels
    AIAA ASCEND 2021 (AIAA 2021-4039)

    A workflow for computing and delivering 10 m-resolution lunar communication coverage maps from terrain masks generated on a supercomputer.

  7. 2020
    In Silico Methods for Space System Analysis: Optical Link Coding Performance and Lunar Terrain Masks
    C. Lee, H. Xie, C.H. Lee, D. Lyakhov, D. Michels
    AIAA ASCEND 2020

    Using KAUST's Shaheen II supercomputer for parallel simulations of deep space optical link coding and for computing lunar terrain masks.

  8. 2018
    Recommendations Emerging from an Analysis of NASA's Deep Space Communications Capacity
    D. Abraham, B. MacNeal, D. Heckman, Y. Chen, J. Wu, K. Tran, A. Kwok, C. Lee
    SpaceOps 2018, Marseille, France

    An analysis of demand on the Deep Space Network's capacity, and the recommendations that came out of it.

  9. 2018
    Complexity-Based Link Assignment for NASA's Deep Space Network for Follow-the-Sun Operations
    J. Lad, M. Johnston, D. Tran, D. Brown, K. Roffo, C. Lee
    SpaceOps 2018, Marseille, France

    Scoring the complexity of each spacecraft link so the three DSN complexes can run the whole network during their local day shifts instead of around the clock.
