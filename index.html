Swarm Command Demo

A toy model of mesh-relayed command and control (C2), inspired by my field work on autonomous maritime swarms, delay-tolerant networking, and comms for the DARPA Subterranean Challenge. It brings together the pieces of a multi-vehicle autonomy system: an operator's C2 node, a degraded radio mesh, and vessels that keep operating on their own when communication links drop out.

From the dashboard you can send orders, jam the radios, or go radio-silent (EMCON). Orders are versioned and relayed hop by hop, every vessel acknowledges back to C2, and the page tracks two operational metrics: time to command (from click until every vessel has confirmed) and time to formation (from click until every vessel is on station).

It runs entirely in the browser on a Three.js scene graph, with a custom GPU shader for the ocean and instanced rendering for the radio traffic. Notes inside the demo walk through the architecture, from the command layer to shaders and GPU buffers.
